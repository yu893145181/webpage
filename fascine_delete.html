Fascine · Delete Account Guide

Step 1
Open Fascine App and go to the Home screen.
Step 2
While logged in, navigate by tapping [Me] → [Personal Information] to enter the Personal Information screen.
Step 3
Tap the [Delete Account] button at the bottom. A confirmation dialog will appear; tap Confirm to permanently delete your account.
⚠️ Account Deletion Notice
  • ▪ Once your account is deleted, all related data will be permanently erased, including:
  •    • Sleep tracking & heart rate monitoring data
  •    • Personal information (avatar, nickname, birthday, gender, etc.)
  • ▪ This action is irreversible — deleted accounts cannot be restored.
  • ▪ If you only wish to sign out, do NOT use "Delete Account". Simply log out from the app settings.
Need help? Contact Fascine support via in-app Help Center.
