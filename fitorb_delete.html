FITORB - Delete Account Guide

Follow the steps below to permanently delete your FITORB account and all associated data.

Step 1

Open the FITORB app and go to the Home screen.
Tap [Me] → [Profile].

Step 2

Inside the [Profile] interface, scroll to the bottom.
Tap [Delete Account].

Step 3

A confirmation pop-up will appear. Tap [Confirm] or [Yes, delete].
Your account will be deleted immediately.

Important Notice

Deleting your account will also permanently remove all related information, including:

  • Email address
  • Nickname
  • Birthday
  • Sleep data
  • Step count and other fitness records

This action is irreversible. Once confirmed, your data cannot be restored.
